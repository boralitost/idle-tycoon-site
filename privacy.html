Privacy

Idle Tycoon privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · One policy for iOS and Android

This policy covers Idle Tycoon, published by Ilyas Bora Altun. The iOS and Android editions are the same game, and the data rules are the same on both stores.

  • iOS, Apple App Store: com.ilyasboraaltun.idletycoon
  • Android, Google Play: com.viogames.secimtycoon

The game is fiction and is not affiliated with real people or political parties. It is not directed at children under 13.

No account

Neither edition asks you to create an account. The game does not ask for an email address, a password, a phone number, or a Google sign-in. The leader name, gender, and province or district you choose stay in the save file on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to a server.

What stays on the device

Campaign progress, buildings, rallies, and store entitlements are stored in the save file on the phone. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, deletes that save on both operating systems.

Advertising

Both editions show ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). That SDK sends the following off the device and shares it with Google and advertising partners:

  • Advertising identifier. On iOS this is Apple’s advertising identifier. On Android it is the Google advertising ID
  • Coarse location derived from the IP address
  • Ad interactions: impressions, clicks, and completion of a rewarded ad
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ads SDK

This data is used to show ads, measure ad performance, and filter invalid clicks. The app has no switch that turns this collection off; it happens when an ad is shown. Those connections use HTTPS. On iOS, Apple asks for permission to track. Refusing that permission does not close the game. On Android, the advertising ID can be reset in the phone’s ads settings.

Google’s own notice: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Gold, VIP, and ad removal are the same products on both stores. On iOS, payment is taken on the Apple App Store screen. On Android, payment is taken on the Google Play screen. Card numbers and bank details never reach the app. The entitlement is stored in the save file on the device. The developer does not write receipts to a server of their own.

What is not shared

The game does not sell data. It does not run its own analytics service or an account server. Unity Analytics is off. This limit is the same on iOS and Android.

Contact

Privacy questions go to the developer, Ilyas Bora Altun: coinminervio@gmail.com
